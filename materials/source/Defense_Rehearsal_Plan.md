## Three run-throughs (not more)

1. **Split run (11/20)** — each section alone, timed. Nobody watches. Goal: your section fits its slot.
2. **Full run (11/22)** — all five in order, one timer, one person noting where numbers disagree with
   the deck. Goal: handoffs and total ≤ 15:00.
3. **Dress rehearsal (11/26–27)** — on the device that will be used, screen mirrored, recording ready.
   Goal: nine steps ×1 clean, and the fallback recording played once to prove it works.

## Pass criteria for the whole defence

- Total 14:30–15:00 with no section over its slot by more than 30 s.
- Every number spoken matches `Defense_Numbers_To_Confirm.md` (confirmed rows only).
- The nine steps run once without a manual fix; if they fail, the recording is switched within 10 s.
- Nobody says a forbidden line: guarantee, optimal, real-time price, AI/ML, accuracy %, FPS.
